# Lyna

Lyna sells and hands out your software from your own Discord server. A license key grants a Discord
role and access to downloads from a Sonatype Nexus, and can be shared or transferred. Licenses come
from Ko-fi sales, PayPal receipts or by hand. A web app gives every product a page, buyers an account
area, and you an admin area.

**Documentation: [docs.chojo.dev/lyna](https://docs.chojo.dev/lyna)**, with hosting, setup and
configuration.

_Trivia: Lyna is the captain of the defensive guard at Crystarium in the critically acclaimed MMORPG Final Fantasy 
XIV, which includes a free trial that includes the entirety of "A Realm Reborn" AND the award-winning "Heavensward" 
expansion up to level 60 with no restrictions on playtime._
